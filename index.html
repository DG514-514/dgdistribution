Trade Terms

Good to Know

Every lot is different — final terms are confirmed on each offer. Here's how we usually work.

Who can buy from DG Distribution?

Wholesale lots are for businesses only: retailers, off-price chains, online sellers, wholesalers and exporters. Individual shoppers can request specific footwear, apparel and bags through Shop Direct.

Is there a minimum order?

Minimums depend on the lot. Most offers are sold by the case or by the pallet. Tell us your typical order size and we'll match offers to it.

What do offer sheets include?

Brand, product type, assortment and size breakdown, quantities, condition, location and price — with photos where available.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide; shipping terms (pick-up, FOB or delivered) are quoted with each lot.

How does payment work?

Payment is by bank wire transfer. Terms are confirmed on each order before shipping.

I'm a brand with excess inventory — how fast can you make an offer?

Send us your stock list and we'll come back quickly with an outright purchase or consignment proposal. All conversations are confidential.
